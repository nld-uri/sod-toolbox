The decomposition

Smooth orthogonal values are 1/ω² when the modes are uncorrelated

Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory · SOD toolbox

Exactness needs modal decorrelation and modal virial balance; for distinct values both are also necessary. The frequency bias is half the virial defect at first order; correlation enters at second order. Example 1: undamped 4-DOF chain, equal modal energies, exact velocities, T = 4000. lambda omega^2 - 1 was 5.2e-4, 3.1e-4, -8.9e-5, 5.0e-5; MAC of smooth modes against LNMs at least 0.99999994. The error follows the residual correlation of the record and does not fall monotonically with its length.
