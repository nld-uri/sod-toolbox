Example 3. 6-DOF chain, zeta 2 %, driven at the last mass by noise band-limited at 2.6 rad/s, 1 % white sensor noise. Central differences: SOD frequencies 0.282, 0.788, 1.385, 3.627, 14.28, 15.76 rad/s; recognition kept only the first. Step 2, lag 5: 0.271, 0.739, 1.167, 1.564, 1.849, 8.43; kept modes 1-4 at peaks 0.270, 0.735, 1.168, 1.566 (true 0.271, 0.741, 1.174, 1.582). Mode 5, weakly excited near the cutoff, mixed with noise and was rejected. A lag of at least 2h+1 removes white noise from both covariances; the pencil may turn indefinite in fast directions, which sort last.
