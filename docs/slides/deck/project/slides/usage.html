Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory · SOD toolbox

Options of sod: V or dt with deriv ('central' step h, or 'gradient'), lag, center, scale, tol, k. Results: mu, freq, sov, Psi, Phi, Q. SMD: W snapshots x points (or x ny x nx), g from make_grid; options density, stiffness, method ('fd', 'whittaker', 'savgol'), kc; results mu, k, omega, Phi, C. Python needs NumPy and SciPy; MATLAB needs no toolboxes. Manual: docs/manual/SOD_manual.pdf.
