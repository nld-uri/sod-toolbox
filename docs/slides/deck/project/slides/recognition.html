Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory · SOD toolbox

A noise direction, a drift or a mixed coordinate can be smoother than a weakly excited mode, so the smoothness order is not the mode order. Spectra are DCT-II periodograms smoothed over a constant relative band, with no wrap-around for non-periodic records. The 15 % width cap stops a drifting coordinate, whose spectrum peaks near zero, from passing. The banded rescue recovers a strong plane contaminated by a neighbor; the energy gate keeps weak narrow-band noise pairs out. Selection for identification and for model reduction are reported separately.
