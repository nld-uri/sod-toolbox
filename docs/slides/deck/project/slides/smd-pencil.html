Smooth mode decomposition

SOD transposed: smooth shapes, unordered snapshots

  • Rows of W are snapshots, columns are points; Gk are spatial derivative fields.
  • Operator from the physics: gradient (strings, membranes, flows), second (beams), plate, Laplacian.
  • Exact iff the true shapes are orthogonal in both the mass and the energy products.

Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory · SOD toolbox

SMD (Li, Wanchoo, Shukla and Chelidze, MSSP 2023) solves the SOD pencil with snapshots in place of channels and spatial derivatives in place of velocities. It is a Rayleigh-Ritz projection of the structure's mass-stiffness pencil onto the span of the snapshots, so snapshot order, timing and amplitudes do not enter. With density in S_W and stiffness in S_G, mu = omega^2 for a self-adjoint structure matched by the operator; with unit weights mu = k^(2m). The pencil itself is older: maximum autocorrelation factors (Switzer and Green 1984; Shapiro and Switzer 1989) and Hooker and Roberts (2016). The toolbox never forms S_W: it solves the pencil on the thin SVD of the weighted field.
