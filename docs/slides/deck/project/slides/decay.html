Example 4. Per-plane damping errors 3.6e-4, -2.7e-4, -5.2e-3 and 8.6e-3: a tilted plane leaks its neighbour's dynamics. The fit on all coordinates at once (a continuous-time least-squares relative of DMD) returned every zeta to 2.4e-14 or better. The trace of a plane fit depends only on the end states, so the estimate needs a record that has decayed. The identity is exact for the chord derivative; with exact qdot and sampled sums the two forms differ by O((|s| dt)^2 / zeta).
