SOD minimises roughness; POD maximises variance

Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory · SOD toolbox

Two-mass chain, masses 1 and 3, springs 1 and 2, equal modal energies. Each covector psi reads a coordinate q = x^T psi; its roughness is mean(qdot^2)/mean(q^2). The extremes are 0.4381 and 1.8643 against omega = 0.4380 and 1.8641. The LNMs meet at 67.9 degrees; the second POD axis is 24.6 degrees from the second LNM, the second smooth mode 0.002 degrees. Smooth modes are orthogonal only in the inverse-covariance metric. The sweep pauses two seconds at each extreme: there the coordinate is one mode alone, a pure sinusoid at omega 1 or omega 2.
