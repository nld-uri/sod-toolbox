Windowed SOD traces frequency-energy dependence along a decay

Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory · SOD toolbox

Two-mass chain with a cubic spring at mass 1, zeta 0.5 %, started where mode 1 is 12 % stiffer than linear. sod_track with windows of 12 linear periods and the symmetric boundary correction. Against the zero-crossing frequency of x1, the median deviation was 4.1e-4 with the correction and 3.2e-3 without; the largest about 3 % in both. Windowed values are equivalent-linear values of the window; the SOD frequency is a mean-square frequency, so harmonics of the orbit raise it.
