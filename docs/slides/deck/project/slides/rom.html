Nonlinear Dynamics Laboratory · SOD toolbox

MTA: modal truncation augmentation, LNMs plus the static load vectors, M-orthonormal. Energy-Galerkin uses the weight blockdiag(K, M). The residual basis fits qdd = Ka q + Ca qd on the r1 smoothest coordinates and adds POD directions of K^-1 M e, the quasi-static displacement driven by the residual force (kind 'k'). Acceleration-level residuals ('a', 'f') were not useful in our nonlinear-beam tests.
